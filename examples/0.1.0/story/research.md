Douyin and Xiaohongshu engagement examples were not collected. The eight accessible primary references below cover the actual sidebar task and silent demonstration format.

## Principles

- Show the existing navigation, the action, then the changed state. S4 and S5 ground the before/after sequence.
- Keep the pointer visible and the sidebar readable. S1, S2 and S3 ground the framing choice.
- End with the useful result. S6 and S7 ground the task-to-result sequence.
- Keep this feature clip smaller than a product tour. S8 provides the contrasting scope.

These are editorial judgments. The sources do not establish that a particular duration or score improves conversion.

## Rubric

R1 first action understandable 20. R2 one outcome 15. R3 before/after and result hold 20. R4 understandable without sound 15. R5 real UI and recognizable task 15. R6 purposeful motion 10. R7 usable 8-second continuous crop 5. Each score is 1 to 5. Contribution is score times weight divided by 5.
